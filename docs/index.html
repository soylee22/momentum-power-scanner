How the composite score is calculated

Every Stage 2 survivor each week is scored by a single number — the composite. It's the equal-weight mean of four within-pool percentile ranks.

4
RSrk

IBD-style Relative Strength

Weighted blend of 3m/6m/9m/12m returns (front-loaded), percentile-ranked across the full universe to 1–99, then re-ranked within survivors.

proxrk

52-week-high proximity

Distance from 52-week high. Closer to the high ranks higher. Penalises faded former leaders.

1yrk

One-year return

Total return over 252 trading days, local currency. Anchors the score in capital gain magnitude.

Krk

K-ratio (path consistency)

Kestner K-ratio: fit log(price) = a + b·t over 252 days, take b / se(b) (trend-slope t-stat). A smooth grind scores high; a one-off spike then chop scores low even if total 1Y return is huge.

Why the K-ratio is there

RS and 1Y both care about how much a stock moved. They do not care about the path. A biotech that was flat for 11 months then doubled in a week can top a pure-return sort. The K-ratio asks whether the climb was consistent. Equal-weighting it with the other three is what keeps one-off P moves from owning the board.
