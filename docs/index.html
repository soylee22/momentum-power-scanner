How the composite score is calculated

Every Stage 2 survivor each week is scored by a single number — the composite. It's the equal-weight mean of three within-pool percentile ranks. Click a term to highlight what it pulls in.
Universe
S&P 500 + FTSE 100 (~601 names) → Stage 2 gate

~120-140 names this week → Composite rank

3
RSrk

IBD-style Relative Strength percentile

  1. For every name in the full universe, compute a weighted blend of 3m, 6m, 9m, 12m total returns: weights 2·r3 + r6 + r9 + r12. Front-loads the recent quarter.
  2. Rank that blend across the universe, scale to 1-99. That's the raw RS rating column.
  3. Then take the percentile within the survivor pool only. That percentile (0-1) is what enters the composite.

Captures multi-horizon momentum strength.

proxrk

52-week-high proximity percentile

  1. Distance from 52-week high: (high52w − price) / high52w. Lower number = closer to high.
  2. Within the survivor pool, rank by negative distance — so closer-to-high gets a higher percentile.

Distinguishes mature breakouts from over-extended runs. A name at the very high gets ~1.0 here; one already 25% off ATH gets penalised.

1yrk

One-year return percentile

  1. Total return over the trailing 252 trading days, local currency.
  2. Rank within the survivor pool, take the percentile.

Anchors the score in actual capital gain magnitude. A name up 80% over a year out-ranks one up 35%, all else equal — but only at the margin, because RS already captures a lot of this.

Why phenomenal 1Y returns can rank lower

The three components are equal-weighted. A name with a stellar 1Y return but extended (high dist-from-high) or with mediocre 3-9m strength will see its proxrk and RSrk sit in the middle of the pool. Average those three and the composite lands lower than its 1Y headline suggests.

Concretely — a Stage 2 survivor up 110% over the year but already 22% off ATH might score: RSrk = 0.55, proxrk = 0.20, 1yrk = 0.90 → composite 0.55. A boring "only" +28% name pinned to its high might score 0.60 / 0.95 / 0.30 → composite 0.62 and rank above it.

This is by design. The scanner is hunting persistent momentum still in trend, not the year's biggest gainers regardless of where they are now.
